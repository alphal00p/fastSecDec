# Ordinary contact quotients and differential coefficients

This milestone constructs a lower-dimensional ordinary contact chart from an
actual verified contact-open production. It does not complete the general
Bierstone–Milman producer, exceptional-boundary history, global maximum-center
gluing, real branch atlas, or closed-face endpoint resolution.

## Native construction

The implementation is in `threshold/resolution/contact/`. It retains the native
producer and candidate, extends the native polynomial variable map, and clears
existing inverse guards with Symbolica rational-polynomial normalization. Native
localized ideal membership proves both the denominator is a unit and the exact
recombination `U*h=N`. Parameters retain their roles and remain inert.

The new graph relation is `N-z*U=0`. The chosen old free coordinate becomes
dependent; `z` becomes free. The existing étale checker verifies all relations,
inverse-guard derivatives, commuting lifts and parameter inertness. An additional
native equality check proves `Delta_new=Delta_old*U*D_i(h)`, identifying precisely
the intended contact open. The graph-normal derivative of `h` is one and its
other free derivatives vanish.

Restriction replaces `z` by zero in relations and guards together, then verifies
the lower-dimensional frame. It never sets the chosen original coordinate to
zero. A native unit-ideal test reports an empty contact open explicitly.

The production `differential_coefficient()` uses the complete inclusive tower
`J_(j+1)=J_j+sum_i D_i(J_j)` and every graph free-axis lift. For `j<d`, the
restricted ideal carries mark `d-j`; the existing marked-sum operation preserves
all mixed products at the common LCM mark. `normal_coefficient()` is separately
retained as a diagnostic. The order-`d` derivative is excluded from both outputs.

The full differential construction follows the boundary-free coefficient
presentation of [Bierstone–Milman 2008, §3.4, Corollary 4.2 and §5(I.A)](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/41043).
Exercise 4.4(1) discusses marked equivalence with a normal-direction presentation
under its hypotheses; it does not assert equality of their ordinary ideals.
No implementation claim of general equivalence testing or center gluing follows.

## Independent controls and limitations

The six controls cover:

- A plane cusp and a second local quotient reaching relative dimension zero.
- Nonlinear contact on a certified open, without selecting a real sheet.
- Rational contact with exact denominator provenance and an explicit inverse-
  guard derivative check; a missing guard is refused.
- Contact over an existing étale circle equation, preserving its relation.
- Normal versus full differential ideals: for `x²+y²+p*w³`, the full result is
  the ideal generated by `y²,p*w³,p*y*w²`, with mark 2. The mixed term is required;
  the normal result is the ideal generated by `y²+p*w³`, with mark 2.
- Empty contact opens, invalid/fresh-role failures, and early/late resource
  stops retaining the source, verified frames, completed layers and counters.

The raw unreduced recursive presentation reaches the generator cap. This is
preserved as `Incomplete`, not accepted as a chart. After independently checking
native equality with the compact quotient basis generated by `y³`, the next
local construction passes. General native quotient/basis normalization remains
necessary before a practical producer loop; individual examples must not be
hand-simplified in production. The marked weight is retained, and this test does
not assert that ignoring it defines a valid BM invariant recursion.

Preflight checks cover marks, mixed-power/count bounds and checker-owned work.
Native F4, rational normalization and matrix internals still require caller-
owned external time/RSS limits. No numerical values or sampling are involved.

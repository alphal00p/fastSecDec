# Dynamic cap and shape investigation

This is the independent mathematical and native-API review for the follow-up
study authorized on 2026-10-10. The completed benchmark remains historical
evidence, not a comparison of individually optimal deformations. New kite
generation uses the normal Horner-ten setting; existing D05 programs retain
their actual Horner-zero settings. No change of endpoint prescription is
authorized: endpoint reduction remains symbolic IBP, including derivatives of
the full Jacobian and local strength.

## What the existing knobs control

The native direction is `v_i=x_i(1-x_i) d_i F`. Write `r=L u`, with
`lambda=S r`. In the polynomial recipe the retained envelope is

```
H = r²/L² + r² ||v||²/R²
    + m_F sum_k B_k r^(2k-2) + sum_U m_U sum_k C_k r^(2k) = 1.
```

The four nonnegative contributions are the cap, displacement, higher causal
terms, and positive-factor terms. Symbolica's native directional Hessian
series constructs `B_k`; native even ray coefficients construct `C_k`. The
direction is held fixed during the auxiliary ray expansion, then its complete
coordinate dependence is restored before physical differentiation. Structural
counts and dimensions do not shrink at faces.

For the polynomial recipe increasing `L` at fixed `R` can only increase the
radius, eventually saturating one of the other terms. Increasing `R` relaxes
the displacement term; it does not remove the causal or positive-factor
bounds. `S` scales the final strength without changing this radius equation.
All three are already runtime inputs with admitted positive `L,R` and
`0<S<1`; changing them requires binding and fresh pilot admission, not
regeneration. The sign-aware recipe replaces the last two contributions by
squares of smooth positive harmful-term bounds. Its regularity is currently
the generation-time rational constant `1/1000`.

The scalar benchmark's common `L=.1,S=.8,R=1` therefore allowed dynamic
`lambda<=.08`, while fixed used `lambda=.1`. It was a common-cap protocol,
not equal displacement or a search for the best cap. D05's selected dynamic
`L=1e-6` came from the recorded preliminary comparisons; larger `L=1` was
already tested there and performed poorly. That observation does not identify
which bound or which large-weight points caused the loss.

Causality controls branches, not estimator variance. A larger allowed radius
can increase `|det J|`, the ratio factors `z_i/x_i`, numerator magnitudes or
cancellations among subtracted terms. Spatial variation introduces
`-i v_i d_j lambda` into the Jacobian and further derivatives into symbolic
IBP. These are possible mechanisms, not an attribution established by the
old variance table. The absolute value of `L` also depends on the retained
normalization of `F`; compare actual displacement and native source identities
across charts rather than treating their raw caps as universal units.

## Focused diagnostic route

The research example's `contour_cap_study/partition.rs` prepares a native
`DynamicEnvelope` from a retained `ChartRecord` and its actual ordered
coordinates, `F`, positive factors and physical bindings. It uses public
Symbolica evaluators and symbolic differentiation for `H`, `H_u`, `H_x`,
`v`, and `Dv`, then the native bracketed root solver on `[0,1]`. It reports
the four shares, root residual, `u H_u`, strength, displacement and
`d_x lambda = -S L H_x/H_u`. Native complex evaluation supplies `F(z)` and
positive factors at the same point. Native `Matrix::det` supplies the full
Jacobian and a frozen-strength comparison. The latter is diagnostic only;
it must never replace the production integrand's Jacobian.

The diagnostic is ordinary floating-point analysis, not a replacement for
the certified native pilot. Matrix entries are calculated in binary64 and
converted to the native 128-bit field for its determinant API; that adds no
input accuracy. The sign-aware expressions are evaluated through their public
mathematical expressions and may be less cancellation-resistant than the
production smooth-positive callback. Any nonfinite value or failed root is
reported as a diagnostic failure, not a zero contribution.

Each report identifies its **base chart** and original coordinates. A residual
kernel can combine several source charts or request the same map on several
subtraction faces. Its aggregate callback strength range must not be
misrepresented as one interior-point strength. All derivatives precede face
restriction; only retained actual face obligations should be replayed. Native
solver work, optional validation and diagnostic replay time are separate from
the production sampling timer.

Source/API inspection and the isolated native proof pass. Both temporary
diagnostic evaluators explicitly use Horner ten, common-pair limit 1000 and
one optimizer core. The ignored probe restores the original saved kite owner,

## Bounded selection and confirmation

Start with existing `L,R,S`, not a new ansatz. For the kite, a useful initial
dynamic ladder is `L=[.03,.1,.3,1,3,10]`, with `S=.8,R=1`, plus a separately
admitted fixed-strength ladder. Runtime's D05 scout spans its historical
`1e-6` choice through large caps on the dominant sector subset. Keep the full
native Laurent vector, covariance, exact policy, coordinate hashes and sample
counts. A sector-subset result is not a full physical-integral estimate.

At the largest weighted finite-vector points, retain actual coordinates,
weights, source/residual IDs, full vectors, precision rescues, native work IDs
and envelope/Jacobian diagnostics. If displacement dominates, a small follow-up
`R=[.25,1,4]` at the selected cap is more informative than extending `L`
indefinitely. If steep strength variation dominates, test
`S=[.5,.8,.95]` before adding another shape parameter. Freeze each follow-up
grid before its new samples; do not merge different lattice sizes or selected
parameter settings into one estimate. Use paired point streams only for
paired comparisons, and distinct fresh seeds to confirm a selected choice.
Two fresh complete replicas/runs give bounded evidence, not a universal
optimality or confidence-coverage claim.

## Conditional shape extension

No new shape parameter is implemented or approved by this review yet. A
positive smooth damping `q(x;theta)<=1` multiplying an already sufficient
`S r(x)` preserves the same deformation direction and homotopy bound. It can
reduce locally excessive movement, but cannot by itself explore beyond the
existing radius. Its full derivatives would have to enter the images,
Jacobian, symbolic endpoint formulas, exact offsets and saved definitions.
Choosing `q` from a few large sampled points would risk overfitting; a proposed
analytic form must first be motivated by the diagnostic neighbourhoods and
then tested on fresh points.

A coordinate-dependent positive displacement budget is another possible
smooth magnitude change, but the current checker treats `R` as one scalar
binding. Merely injecting such a function into the evaluator would make its
certificate and descriptor inconsistent. Likewise the sign-aware regularity
is retained as an exact constant in the checker. Neither is a free runtime
knob in today's artifact. Any later extension must change generation,
descriptor/cache identity, binding admission and the native checker together,
while preserving the root's nonnegative coefficients and simple-root premise.
Unconstrained direction changes are unnecessary for the initial study.

One conditional envelope extension worth evaluating if the observed dominant
points show large map derivatives is `H_theta=H+theta*r²*||Dv||_F²`, with
`theta>=0`. This is a nonnegative smooth additional magnitude constraint,
not a different direction. It preserves the existing sufficient bound and
simple positive root, and gives `lambda*||Dv||_F<=S/sqrt(theta)` for positive
theta. It does **not** bound `v` times the gradient of lambda, and its own
coordinate derivatives may introduce stiffness. It therefore remains a
mathematical proposal contingent on the data, not an implemented or proven
variance improvement. Native symbolic derivatives, the root coefficient
program, descriptor/checker and saved/runtime identity would all need to
include theta consistently before any use.

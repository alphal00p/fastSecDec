# Independent review of the named-coefficient actual-target proposal

The proposed bounded experiment in `native-named-actual-proposal.md` has no
scientific ownership blocker, subject to the concrete source checks below.
The small symbolic, program, weighted and cold-reader controls have already
passed; they do not establish this larger representative. This note is a design
review; subsequent source acceptance and the failed bounded execution are
recorded below.

The original mapped terms and the independent Taylor expression must be bound
to the same immutable capture: zero-based representative 80, chart 119,
multiplicity four, nine coordinates, and the retained 2,112/1,026 geometry and
representative counts. Reuse of the completed 872-piece Taylor identity from
the failed IBP preparation is valid: that identity completed before IBP began.
It supplies no completed IBP expression or numerical oracle. Canonical native
term identities plus the original expression/capture hashes must be checked;
the candidate must not recreate the 137-MB expression just to establish input
identity. Multiplicity stays descriptive and is never applied to either side.

The independent oracle starts with the original epsilon-dependent Taylor
expression, substitutes one prescribed exact rational point, and then invokes
native series. It neither imports named coefficients nor uses their pole order
or remainder to choose its own result. Native relative-width retries must prove
an absolute remainder above zero before zero gaps are admitted. Each result
retains the actual lowest order through the finite coefficient and requires
512/1024-bit stability. Candidate comparison uses the union of both available
order sets, including extra poles and native zero gaps. Unavailable or timed-out
oracle points remain unavailable; a subset cannot be reported as the full
three-point gate.

Separate named-generation, program-build and cold-reader stages provide useful
failure attribution. Ordinary native Atom exports for roots, handles and bodies
are diagnostic transport, not a new production codec. The builder must check
unique handle identities, consistent shared definitions and all hashes before
registering one common native alias map. It must reject unresolved regulators
or formal coefficient/derivative requests. Exact IR remains the native owner
for O2, conditioning, MPFR and reload. No registry or callback may be required by
the cold reader, and no implicit physical-body restoration is needed.

The additional cancellation guard needs one precise qualification. Its axis
bounds must come from all original mapped endpoint powers through the existing
native endpoint-power rule, not just maxima of surviving Taylor rows: an
exactly pruned branch may be absent from that retained list. Check that the
bound dominates every original row and use checked addition for the resulting
total degree. Preserve original rows separately from the added conservative
row. On the open unit cube, each `-log2(x_i)` is nonnegative, so a componentwise
larger row can only increase the native lost-bit bound and request earlier
precision checks. This is a scheduling safeguard, not a cancellation identity,
an all-domain bound or evidence of coefficient equality. It does not change
weights, means, coefficients or oracle tolerances.

Exact rational comparisons and weighted production checks remain distinct.
The latter must use MPFR at the same rounded binary64 coordinates and weights
as the actual production call, retaining both ordinary and forced replay
reports. All three point readers remain separately bounded at 180 seconds plus
grace and 30-GiB address space. A successful result would cover this single
representative at the specified points plus the reviewed native composition
contract; it would not establish a complete graph, integration, convergence or
speedup. Concrete source and frozen-runner review remain required before launch.

## Concrete ignored source review

`generate`, `program` and `reader` modules, and the narrow native endpoint-power
adapter has no scientific source blocker. It binds imported canonical mapped

digest, and requires native absolute remainder above zero. The cancellation
guard derives all nine bounds from the mapped powers, checks dominance of the
retained rows and adds its total with checked arithmetic.

or function requests. The reader requires all three source-bound original

record, the exact IR and program record, and all oracle records/source inputs.

manifest lists every imported file. This coverage check belongs to concrete
runner review. No source or outcome is accepted merely because the manifest
has a nonzero number of entries. Compilation and actual execution remain
separate gates.

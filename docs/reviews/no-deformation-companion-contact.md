The component residual-order result now feeds the existing native contact and
coefficient operations on its actual upper-order opens. The implementation
shares the previously reviewed principal-open geometry and history checker;
it does not introduce another derivative engine, quotient ring or CAS.

Each open retains its exact cover owner, index and denominator-clearing
witness. Its cover may describe only the marked cosupport. Such an open does
not certify coverage of the physical integration domain. Restriction retains
the original history, boundary identities and frozen old-boundary snapshot.

On a positive-order open, the pulled companion's actual order must agree with
its mark before constructing maximal contact. A smaller order returns a
retained lower-priority region; a larger one contradicts the upper-order proof
and is rejected. The resulting coefficient operation uses the same native frame
owner as the restricted history. It preserves all old-boundary terms.

quotients are pulled back together. Unit boundary factors are absorbed before
the existing monomial-center checker verifies recombination and a unit
residual. Empty algebraic opens, empty marked cosupport and lower-order regions
never become zero physical contributions.

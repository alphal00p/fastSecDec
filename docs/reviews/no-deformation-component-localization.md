## Native reuse and execution

The implementation reuses the existing native component split, quotient
normalization, ideal membership, `VerifiedOpenCover`, checked localization,
support embedding, common-frame join, center ascent and standard blow-up.
It adds construction-bound orchestration, not a CAS or alternate transform.
The coordinator and an independent kernel agent reviewed these boundaries.

`ComponentLocalizationFrontier` is caller-stepped. It retains every issued
open, leaves accepted states unchanged on retry, and preserves unresolved
nonzero/nonunit children. Invalid evidence remains an error. Completion means
a complete local outcome inventory, not a resolved integration domain.
Progress is in memory; durable recovery and aggregate RSS are separate gates.

The initial scope admits one retained contact level. Higher stacks, enclosing
physical ascent, overlap gluing and the pending physical guard refinements
remain explicit work. This does not close G0, the complete G3 driver or G5.

# Recursive BM driver: implementation review boundary

The producer and root reviewed the proposed recursion against
[Bierstone–Milman 2008, §§5 and 7.2](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/41043).
The registered implementation includes reusable first-center recursion and its
first carried continuation at fixed parameters. Acceptance of these local
stages is not acceptance of the full global resolver.

`BoundaryFreeFirstCenter` now obtains the native maximum order, retains checked
quotient normalization, and descends through the full differential coefficient
object. It selects a contact candidate only when its derivative is a unit on
the entire current localization. Otherwise it reports that multiple contact
opens are required. Each return lifts the center through its actual contact
embedding, clears only verified units, checks two-way ideal equality on
restriction, and verifies admissibility in the original parent source.

The optional first coordinate blow-up proves that the generated center ideal
is exactly the proposed coordinate ideal before constructing every standard
pivot. It retains controlled-transform and target-order evidence. An unresolved
target explicitly requires later boundary cycles; it cannot re-enter the
empty-boundary producer and discard its new exceptional divisor. Top-level
zero ideals, nonlinear centers, resource stops and missing contact covers have
separate outcomes. Accepted recursion steps are atomic in-memory states, not
durable checkpoints.

Root and an independent runtime/mathematics agent reviewed this slice. Four new
isolated test groups pass alongside the existing 73 resolver controls, with
strict Clippy. They derive the marked cusp's center and both blow-up charts,
retain all generators of a nonprincipal ideal, refuse to declare the mark-one
cusp resolved, verify a Cartier control and exercise retry/admission boundaries.
The ratio vector contains finite entries only; terminal infinity is implicit
in the completed center outcome. No global termination claim follows from it.
The joined threshold suite passes 156 tests (one ignored child entry), with
strict library/test Clippy on the adopted dependency graph.

## First carried continuation

The next registered stage connects the actual first all-pivot blow-up to the
geometric history. It carries the original companion, contact support and
coefficient ideal through the same checked map, retaining exact controlled
quotients and source-generator associations. Coordinate-center provenance is
typed separately from boundary-index centers. No synthetic exceptional IDs or
restart with an empty geometric history is used.

The continuation checks that the old companion has empty cosupport and that
the current component's residual maximum strictly decreases. Only that private
proof permits a new recursive-cycle snapshot. The snapshot records the new
cycle's old divisors while preserving the original geometric root, exceptional
births and chart ancestry. Its companion coefficient then drives the existing
lower-dimensional recursion and returns the next checked lifted center.
Deferred lower-incidence opens remain in the proof frontier.

Native quotient normalization is now applied after contact restriction and
before large marked powers. Raw derivative layers and normalization receipts
remain available; subsequent ambient derivatives still use the unrestricted
layer. A `y^2+x*y` control detects the invalid alternative of differentiating
only after restriction. A separate cusp control distinguishes carried `C`
from freshly recomputed derivatives of the transformed ideal, even when their
marked cosupports are both empty.

Root and the independent runtime agent reviewed the construction, history,
order checks and Symbolica reuse. Six new isolated groups pass alongside all
83 resolver tests, with strict Clippy. They cover nonempty boundaries including
degree five, principal/nonprincipal inputs, both first pivots, a residual
maximum decrease from two to one, new-cycle recursion, owner substitutions and
atomic resource retry. Native polynomial division, unit clearing, exact ideal
normalization and the existing relative derivative frames perform the algebra.
After registration, the joined native threshold suite passes 162 tests with one
ignored child entry (10.51 seconds); strict library/test Clippy and formatting
also pass on the adopted dependency graph.

The accepted carry path requires polynomial-exact quotients in its first frame.
General quotient-support division, noncoordinate/relative second transitions,
global center gluing and a complete real integration atlas remain unfinished.
A local residual drop does not prove a global maximum or a completed divisor
resolution. Native internal allocation still requires caller-owned hard limits.

## Remaining general-driver obligations

The proposed state separates the ambient atlas, embedded smooth support,
marked presentation, exceptional-divisor history and recursive cycle. It uses
exact normalized order/incidence pairs with explicit terminal values. Center
priority includes the ordered exceptional subset; descent uses the invariant
and auxiliary order. A generic rational tuple comparator alone proves no
termination. Coefficient recursion, parent-order drops, incidence drops and the
monomial stage must follow their respective theorem-backed transitions.

Every recursive coefficient object retains its embedding into the parent
ambient space. Returning a center therefore requires a checked lift, rather
than treating a contact quotient as the original ambient space. Both source
generator associations and the original coefficient transform survive a
blow-up. Recomputing derivatives afterwards is not a substitute for carrying
that transform. Incidence cycles preserve the old-boundary snapshot; a verified
residual-order drop authorizes a new snapshot.

The review requires a distinction between construction derivations and actual
marked-equivalence edges. An arbitrary marked sum is not equivalent to either
input. In particular, adding the old-boundary term restricts the relevant
cosupport. Only checked hypotheses of an applicable equivalence theorem can
create an equivalence edge.

The first control must derive the marked cusp's coefficient recursion, lift
its terminal center and verify both blow-up charts through reusable driver
state. A hard-coded sequence is insufficient. Resolving `(y^2-x^3,2)` is an
intermediate gate; mark-one principalization, nonprincipal ideals and later
exceptional cycles remain required. Alternate maximal-contact choices must
produce compatible lifted centers on actual overlap rings.

Global delivery further requires finite ambient overlap/cocycle owners,
general smooth-center adaptation, exact strict-transform/graph closure,
globally shared exceptional identities, and checked center gluing. Equal
cosupport or equal sampled values do not prove equal scheme centers. Deferred
lower maxima and geometric zero components remain represented explicitly.

The absolute characteristic-zero theorem does not automatically establish
simultaneous resolution over inert parameters. Relative centers, ranks, order
conditions and transforms need specialization-compatible strata. For example,
the marked family `(x^2-p,2)` cannot be resolved by silently mixing `p` into an
integration blow-up. General parameter stratification, the real integration
atlas and endpoint/regulator certificates remain open delivery gates.

# Construction-bound nonmonomial lower recursion

This local resolver extension carries an actual nonmonomial lower problem
through a shared physical blow-up. It does not complete the general
Bierstone–Milman driver or certify an integration atlas.

## Source, history and cover checks

`InitialProblem` is issued only from an original coefficient construction or a
checked incidence drop. Private constructors retain the actual source, history
root, stage and exceptional births. A current transformed ideal cannot be
substituted retroactively as an original problem. Subsequent cycles still
require their proved residual-order drop.

The original coefficient hierarchy is captured before a physical pivot. It
retains each source, normalization, contact, child and full center restriction,
with cumulative embeddings and original history roots. All levels use the
same physical blow-up. Common refinements retain every nonempty and empty
support intersection, full ideal and inverse-guard equations, checked maps and
relative chain rules. Parameters remain inert; extra inverse slots do not
become physical integration coordinates.

A lower open descends directly only when its cleared factors are already units
on the physical ambient chart. Otherwise an explicit physical cover is needed.
The checked cover proves coverage of the exact marked cosupport, then includes
the derivative-ideal complementary opens and proves full ambient coverage.
A complementary open establishes emptiness of that particular marked
cosupport, not a zero density or an ancestor's residual-order drop.

The new caller-stepped recursion retains all admitted initial-problem outcomes.
Its `complete_inventory()` means inventory completion, not completed resolution.
Root review tightened the zero-ideal outcome: it retains the complete factor
inventory and repeatedly reports incomplete work. The regression constructs
that case from the genuine smooth parent `(x,1)` and its zero coefficient
ideal; it never manufactures private proof objects.

## Native reuse and public API

Public methods consume checked owners: original/incidence `initial_problem()`,
`InitialProblem::recursion()`, caller `advance()`, prepared-center `ascend()`,
original-tree `pull_first()` and `histories()`, and physical-cover operations.
Raw construction fields remain private. A separately linked consumer exercises
these APIs without crate-private access.

API, source and executable probes reuse Symbolica polynomial remapping and
ideal membership, the existing relative Etale derivatives, normalization,
unit clearing, verified open covers and controlled-support transforms. One
shared frame-joining helper handles both support restrictions and nested
refinements. No separate CAS, AD, graph type, sampler or scheduling pool is
introduced. Root and an independent agent reviewed these ownership and
scientific boundaries before import.

hierarchy preserves seven lower histories and nine empty-support receipts.
